Frequently Asked Questions

How does FoodSaver work?

Restaurants update their leftover food inventory live, and users can book and pick up meals through the app.

Is FoodSaver free to use?

Yes, users can download and use the app for free. Restaurants pay a nominal fee for advanced analytics and insights.
